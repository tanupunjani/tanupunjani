## 🧠 Healthcare analytics and research leadership

### Electronic Frailty Index — end-to-end technical ownership

Designed and developed the computational implementation of a **71-deficit Electronic Frailty Index** for an approximately **27,521-patient research cohort**, using SQL, Python, and structured Epic EHR data.

- Translated clinical research definitions into extraction, scoring and denominator logic using diagnoses, laboratory results and flowsheets.
- Handled missing observations, time-dependent definitions, edge cases and analytical validation; documented decisions and tested output consistency.
- Owned the technical path from initial specifications and data discovery through implementation, quality assurance, research handoff and iteration.
- Shared implementation code with a Hamilton Health Sciences researcher to support a corresponding frailty-index development effort.

**Research project, not a claimed clinical deployment.** Institutional source code and patient-level information are not public.

### QT-MACE — clinical analytics, statistical modeling and ML research

Lead the analytical/ML research workstream examining QT-prolonging medication exposures and major adverse cardiovascular outcomes, with research data involving approximately **150,000 patients** (reported project scale), alongside research statisticians, physicians, and McMaster University collaborators.

- Develop research cohorts and analytical datasets; resolve medication, event-timing, quality and data-definition challenges across complex EHR sources.
- Work with regression and gradient-boosting methods and explore clinical NLP and LLM-assisted text-extraction methods in **research settings**.
- Work end to end with clinical researchers: study discovery → data acquisition and cleaning → statistical analysis → model exploration → interpretation and review.
- Provide guidance and training to research and co-op students on data cleaning, statistics, validation, reproducible analysis and responsible clinical AI experimentation.

**No claim of a deployed predictive model or production LLM clinical system.**

## 🌿 Featured build — PD Bridge

**Accessible, privacy-conscious technology for people living with Parkinson's and their care partners.**

[![PD Bridge — iPhone prototype with fictional data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tanupunjani/pd-bridge-ios/main/screenshots/home.png)](https://tanupunjani.github.io/pd-bridge-case-study/)

I independently designed and developed PD Bridge through a browser prototype and a separate SwiftUI iPhone implementation. It explores how everyday observations can be captured more comfortably and organized for an appointment without overwhelming the person using it.

**What I've built and explored:**

- **Product research and UX:** accessibility-first information architecture, reduced-friction capture, Comfort Mode and user walkthroughs.
- **Development:** browser workflows and a native SwiftUI iPhone app, with manual observation and event capture, editable history, voice-note review and appointment report preparation.
- **Privacy engineering:** local AES-GCM encrypted vault, app-private storage, device-protected keys, and optional passphrase-encrypted export.
- **Quality and iteration:** code-level tests, accessibility considerations, fictional-data scenarios and documented limitations.
- **Creative direction:** visual identity, experience design, interaction details and product storytelling.

**Explore the project:**

- **[Full design and engineering case study](https://tanupunjani.github.io/pd-bridge-case-study/)**
- **[Public iPhone source code](https://github.com/tanupunjani/pd-bridge-ios)**
- **[Case-study source repository](https://github.com/tanupunjani/pd-bridge-case-study)**
- [Privacy policy](https://tanupunjani.github.io/pd-bridge-case-study/privacy.html) · [Support page](https://tanupunjani.github.io/pd-bridge-case-study/support.html)

*PD Bridge is a pre-release, fictional-data prototype, not a clinically validated or App Store-released medical product. It does not provide live AI, automatic fall detection, clinical monitoring, or diagnosis; experimental Watch code is not embedded in the current iPhone Release app.*
